How this works

Search — name a film or a series to see if we have it, or describe a mood ("something gentle after a long week"). You pick what you like and add it to a playlist.

Make a playlist from this — skips the picking. Describe the list you want ("anything great for the big screen") and it builds it in your Plex straight away.

If we don't have it — you'll be offered a button to ask for it. It goes on the wanted list, and you'll get a notification when it turns up.

Your playlists are yours — they appear in your own Plex app, on any device.

About the AI — describing a mood uses a bit of AI to work out what to look for. There's a daily allowance so it can't run up a bill. Searching by name is free and always works, allowance or not.
