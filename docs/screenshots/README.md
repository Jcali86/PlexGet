# Screenshots

Empty on purpose. The page carries your library, your app name, your persona
and whichever skin you picked, so a set of somebody else's screenshots would be
showing a stranger an app that does not exist.

Put yours here and link them from the Screenshots section of the top-level
`README.md`. Worth capturing, roughly in the order somebody meets them:

- the sign-in, on a phone
- a search that found something, posters and all
- a request for a film the library does not have
- the wanted list
- the owner console at `/dashboard`

Two things to check before committing an image: no real usernames in the
header or on a playlist, and no `X-Plex-Token` in a URL bar.
