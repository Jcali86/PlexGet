## Goal

Add `Store` (the StoreKit 2 wrapper implementing `Entitlements`, `OwnerEntitlement`, `Products.storekit`), server-key issuance at the relay and its forwarding to `POST /api/v1/owner/relay`, "Unlink and delete my relay data", and the Console tab (Requests, Inbox, Announce, Discover, Overview, Notifications, Server, Sessions, Paywall) behind `ConsoleGate`, so that the owner's purchase of `owner.yearly` or `owner.monthly` opens the Console and turns the household's push delivery to `relay`, a lapse closes the Console and leaves every household screen untouched, restore works, and the relay refuses a forged JWS through the real verifier. Purchase, lapse, restore and grace are proven by StoreKit testing in `xcodebuild test` and by one sandbox purchase on a device.

## Constraints

- An entitlement decision is made only on a `VerificationResult.verified` transaction; every verified transaction is finished; `Transaction.currentEntitlements` at launch, one `Transaction.updates` listener for the app's lifetime, `Product.SubscriptionInfo.Status`: `.subscribed` and `.inGracePeriod` count as active; `.inBillingRetryPeriod`, `.expired` and `.revoked` do not (Apple `Product.SubscriptionInfo.RenewalState` documentation: only subscribed and grace entitle the customer; counting billing retry would open the Console for up to 60 days past the relay's cut-off), `AppStore.sync()` behind "Restore purchases".
- The Console unlocks by StoreKit only: no code, no link, no mention of the web dashboard, a website or any other purchase path anywhere in `App/`. The household screens stay free for the owner too, including gifting and "for whom".
- `ConsoleGate` is the only type that answers `isVisible` (role `owner`, or `client.isDemo`) and `isOpen` (`client.isDemo`, or `OwnerEntitlement.active`); the app never presents Paywall on its own, only as the Console tab's root.
- The relay sees a JWS; the PlexGet server never sees a receipt or a JWS, only `{server_key, relay_url, expires_at}` on `POST /api/v1/owner/relay`; the `id` from `POST /v1/server-keys` goes to the Keychain under `relay.server-key-id.<machine_id>` and nowhere else.
- Every `URLRequest`, including `POST /v1/server-keys` and `DELETE /v1/server-keys/<id>`, is built in `PlexGetKit/Sources/PlexGetKit/ServerAPI/Endpoints.swift`; `URLSession` only in `App/Session/URLSessionTransport.swift`; `openURL` only in the folders of 09 §11's allow-list (`Report`, `Paywall`, `Welcome`, `Bell`, `Pairing`, `Settings`), with `AppIdentity.privacyPolicyURL`, `AppIdentity.termsURL` and "Restore purchases" being what Paywall opens.
- Notifications is read-only: `PUT /api/v1/owner/notifications` is cut and no builder, string or control for it exists.
- Forbidden in this phase (07 §5 Phase 7): an entitlement decision on a transaction that is not `VerificationResult.verified`; an unlock that is not StoreKit; an open Console outside `DemoServerClient`; `PUT /api/v1/owner/notifications`; any call to `/wanted/export`, `/export*`, `/sonarr/*`, `/radarr/*`; editing a household screen except to add the Console tab; a receipt or JWS reaching the server.

## Do not

- Do not grant `OwnerEntitlement.active` from an `.unverified` result, a cached flag, a `UserDefaults` value, a server field or a demo switch; only a verified StoreKit transaction or `DemoServerClient` opens the Console.
- Do not add any unlock path that is not StoreKit: no code entry, no link to a website, no sentence mentioning the web dashboard or a purchase made elsewhere.
- Do not send a receipt, a JWS or an `originalTransactionId` to the PlexGet server, and do not send the server key anywhere but `POST /api/v1/owner/relay`.
- Do not present Paywall from any screen but the Console tab's root, and do not show it on launch, after sign-in or on a timer.
- Do not build or call `PUT /api/v1/owner/notifications`, `GET /reports/gaps`, `/wanted/export`, `/export*`, `/sonarr/*` or `/radarr/*`; Notifications shows the catalogue read-only.
- Do not change a household screen beyond adding the Console tab and the owner-only badges; every member screen must render identically to Phase 5.
- Do not type a price, a currency or a trial length into code or a string; the Paywall reads them from `Product` at run time.
- Do not create `App/Session/RelayClient.swift` or any `URLRequest` outside `Endpoints.swift`; extend the Kit's `RelayClient`.
- Do not mark the sandbox purchase as done; it is the owner's device step, and the phase does not pass without the two pasted bodies.
- Do not skip a `StoreTests` case that fails under the configuration; a StoreKit behaviour that differs from 04 §3.3 is a deviation note.
